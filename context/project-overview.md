# Qrate — Project Overview

> Planning documentation. Product decisions are recorded below; these notes are not a claim that the features already exist in code.

## Product definition

Qrate (Qrate.fun) is a video-first Solana speculation platform. Qrate hosts short-form videos. Viewers discover videos and buy or sell video-associated tokens to speculate on their potential popularity; token prices reflect market trading activity, not a direct formula based on view counts. Creators earn a share of fees generated by trading their videos' tokens.

## User groups

- **Viewers / traders:** Watch videos, discover creators, interact socially, trade video tokens using their own Solana wallets, and inspect portfolios.
- **Creators:** Upload videos, maintain drafts, publish eligible content, launch associated tokens where applicable, earn trading fees, review video and trading analytics, and request deletion.
- **Moderators / administrators:** Review content and deletion requests, address appeals and reports. Moderation tooling and exact access policy remain TBD.

## Video product decisions

- Maximum video length: **60 seconds** (MVP).
- Accept vertical, horizontal, and square videos; preserve aspect ratios without stretching inside a consistent **9:16** player. Cropping or letterboxing to be designed.
- MVP: upload existing videos; **in-app recording/editing deferred**.
- Allow **free private drafts** with storage limits TBD; payment should not occur before relevant upload and publishing checks.
- MVP feed: **For You, Trending, Following**, with simple initial ranking and later refinement.
- MVP social: **likes, comments, shares, follows, bookmarks**.
- Captions/descriptions and hashtags supported. External links restricted to verified creator profiles under an exact policy TBD.
- Video content is immutable after publication (no replacing the video); description edits permitted, subject to anti-abuse and metadata integrity rules.
- Video thumbnail generated automatically. Custom thumbnails and manual frame selection are **post-MVP possibilities**, not commitments.
- Qrate hosts video content rather than embedding TikTok/Reels as the sole playback source. Creators may repost their own content and provide cross-platform links where rights allow.

### CRITICAL OPEN DECISION: video publication vs token creation

**TBD.** An earlier decision required every public video to launch its unique token simultaneously. This requirement was **reopened**. Qrate may first publish/review videos and allow token launches afterward to avoid stranded tokens for unsuitable content. Whether tokenless videos enter the public feed, who triggers delayed launch, and which moderation checks are required **have not been decided**.

- For tokenized videos, a video is associated with **one new, unique Solana token**, not an existing token. This is the current intended token model, conditional on the eventual publishing sequence.
- Never label a token as launched until creation has been verified on-chain.
- Do not launch tokens automatically before required screening.

## Token metadata

- Video title supplied by creator; token name initially derived from the title, but editable **before token launch**.
- Ticker/symbol generated automatically; it need not be central to Qrate's user interface.
- Video thumbnail becomes token image; caption can supply token description.
- Treat token metadata as immutable after launch for Qrate's product flow; verify precise on-chain authorities and locking configuration.

## Trading experience

- SOL is the primary trading currency; show approximate USD equivalent.
- Meteora **Dynamic Bonding Curve (DBC)** before graduation and the configured post-graduation Meteora pool, expected **DAMM v2**. **No PumpSwap integration in scope.**
- Simple **market-swap** trading for MVP. Limit orders, leverage, and complex order types are not committed to MVP; advanced trading remains a discussion topic.
- Swipe vertically to change videos. **Swipe right** or tap **Trade** to open that video's separate trading tab. Tap creator avatar/name to open their profile. Detailed trading-tab layout is deferred.
- Trading controls: configurable slippage (automatic/manual), quick buy presets **0.1 / 0.5 / 1 SOL** plus custom amount, transaction preview (estimated quantity/fees/price impact), failure handling, and token trading history.
- Personal trader portfolio: holdings, value, realized/unrealized PnL, and trade history. Calculations and limitations must be disclosed.
- Creator dashboard: views/watch time, engagement, token market cap/volume, token holder count, fees earned, withdrawals. Detailed dashboard UX and accounting are deferred.

## Creators and verification

- Anyone may create a draft. Token launch requires payment of relevant costs or deposit under a pricing model still being finalized.
- Creators may link TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube accounts and original video URLs.
- Profile verification may combine account-ownership proof, external verification, and follower thresholds. **Precise criteria TBD**; avoid presenting a Qrate badge as external endorsement.

## Deletion and removal policy

- Creators **request** deletion; Qrate reviews/approves rather than allowing immediate unilateral deletion of public videos.
- Qrate may separately remove content required by law or content policy.
- Removing a video does **not** delete an existing Solana token or its transaction history. Trading may remain possible on-chain even if Qrate hides or disables its own interface.
- Preserve the associated token page, chart, and transaction history where technically and legally appropriate.
- Placeholder headline: **“What happens on blockchain stays on blockchain...”**
- Explain that the video was removed, while the token remains on Solana; show a standard public reason (creator request, copyright, guidelines, legal requirements) without exposing private moderation details.
- Warn creators and traders before launch/purchase that videos can be removed but blockchain tokens cannot be erased.

## Economics — working planning assumptions

- **Trading fee proposal:** **1.9%** for buys and sells. Fees may differ after graduation; validate against live Meteora configuration.
- **Revenue split intention:** **60% Qrate / 40% creator** of the agreed *distributable* fee revenue, **after applicable protocol deductions**; verify exactly how Meteora implements this for DBC and DAMM v2. Do not accidentally interpret 60/40 as a split of 100% of the gross fee.
- **Launch costs/deposit:** Actual Solana/DBC creation cost must be calculated from transactions. A refundable launch **deposit / bail** is a proposal: potentially return eligible funds after Qrate recoups its launch expenditure. Amount (earlier $5 example), mechanism, eligibility, funding source, and anti-abuse safeguards are **TBD**. On-chain rent/fees are not automatically refundable.
- **MVP operating spend:** Rough planning budget of **around $200/month**; one illustrative estimate was **$244/month** ($150 video, $49 Helius, $25 Supabase, $20 Railway). These are not vendor quotes or verified current invoices.
- Primary revenue from trading activity; launch markup, payout thresholds, and revenue economics require validation.

## MVP vs later/TBD

**MVP intended:** short videos, adaptive playback, free drafts, simple discovery, social features, wallet-based swaps, market statistics/charts, portfolio, creator earnings/dashboard, and necessary safety/reliability measures — subject to staged development and unresolved publishing decisions.

**Post-MVP agreed/deferred:** in-app recording/editing, paid subscriptions, automated creator verification, video boosting/paid promotion, custom thumbnail editing.

**Not finalized:** live streaming, direct messaging, advanced order types, embedded wallets, fiat on-ramp, exact video provider, moderation workflow, verification criteria, publish-before-token sequencing, detailed UI, launch deposit design.

## Legal and distribution gate

EU MiCA/CASP classification, token-offering obligations, content rights, and Apple's cryptocurrency and UGC App Store requirements need qualified review before public EU/iOS launch. Use of Meteora, a non-custodial wallet, or Pump.fun as a reference is **not** itself regulatory approval.
